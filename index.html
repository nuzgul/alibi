Alibi privacy policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Alibi (Photo Vault Calculator) is a private photo and video vault for Android, made by Hanoon Labs. Your photos, videos and their details are encrypted and stored only on your phone. Alibi has no account, no cloud and no server, and never uploads your media. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob; Google may collect device data for those ads.

Data that stays on your phone

  • Photos and videos you add, their thumbnails, original file names, dates, albums and trash state. They are encrypted on your device (AES-256). We never receive them.
  • Your PIN and recovery code are never stored in readable form; only encrypted key material derived from them is kept on the device.
  • Failed-attempt alerts (optional, Pro): only the time and number of wrong PINs, encrypted on the device. Alibi never takes photos of anyone.
  • Settings such as auto-lock time, language, backup reminder and app-icon choice.
  • Pro status: whether Pro is active and when Google Play last confirmed it.

Uninstalling the app deletes all of this. Android's own backup is turned off for Alibi; the only way to keep a copy is an encrypted backup file that you create and store where you choose.

Data you choose to send out

  • Backups: an encrypted .vault file saved to a place you pick (for example Google Drive, a computer or a USB drive). It can be opened with your recovery code, and with your PIN if your PIN has at least 6 digits.
  • Share / Unhide: when you share an item or copy it back to your gallery, a decrypted copy goes to the app or folder you choose. That copy is then outside Alibi's protection.

Advertising (free version)

Alibi uses Google AdMob to show a banner on the vault home screen and rewarded ads when you choose to watch one. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect your IP address (approximate location), app interaction data, diagnostics and device identifiers such as the advertising ID, and share them with Google for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), you are asked for consent first, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → “Ad privacy choices”. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings. Ads never have access to your vault content.

Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy · How Google uses data from apps: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Purchases

Alibi Pro is sold through Google Play. Payment details are handled by Google; we never receive them. Alibi only asks Google Play whether Pro is active and stores that result on your device.

Permissions

  • Photos and videos: to import the items you choose and, if you ask, delete the originals (Android asks you to confirm).
  • Camera: only inside Alibi's own camera, when you open it.
  • Notifications (optional): backup reminders.
  • Biometrics (optional): fingerprint or face unlock. Alibi never stores biometric data.
  • Internet: ads and Google Play only.

Children

Alibi is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country).

Security

Encryption protects your data if someone copies the app's files, but no app can fully protect a phone that is rooted, infected with malware, or used by someone else while the vault is open. Use a PIN of 6 to 8 digits and keep your recovery code safe: without the PIN or the recovery code, nobody (including us) can open your vault.
